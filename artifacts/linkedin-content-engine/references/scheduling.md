## Special modes

- Ramadan: use 9:30 PM–1:30 AM post-taraweeh as prime and 2:00–3:30 PM pre-iftar as secondary. Do not post near iftar/maghrib.
- Eid, Founding Day, and National Day: celebration content can post on the day; hold technical content until two days later.

## Cadence

Post 2–3 times weekly, leave 36–48 hours between posts, and never post twice in one day.
